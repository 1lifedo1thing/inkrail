Inkrail is an offline-first writing app. No account, no developer-operated cloud for your letters. Your writing lives in a library folder you control.
01 / YOUR WRITING

Information the desktop app handles

To let you write, edit, organize, import, and export letters, Inkrail accesses the text and formatting you create or choose to import, letter titles, and the files in your selected local library. It also stores app preferences, such as appearance and sound settings, and information needed to remember the library location.

Inkrail does not require you to register, provide a name or email address, or create an online account. The app does not send your writing to the Inkrail developer for storage or processing. Inkrail does not operate an advertising or behavioral analytics service within the app, and we do not sell your writing or personal information.

02 / LOCAL STORAGE

Where your writing is saved

Inkrail saves its library as local app-managed files. Its internal letter library is not simply a folder of Markdown files; you can export individual letters as Markdown (.md) or plain text (.txt). The default locations are:

MACOS~/Library/Application Support/Inkrail/
WINDOWS%APPDATA%\Inkrail\Library

You can choose another storage folder in Inkrail's settings. The app copies the library when moving it and leaves the old folder in place as a safety copy. You are responsible for managing any copies or backups you keep.

03 / KEEP OR REMOVE

Deletion and recovery

Deleted letters remain in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days and can be restored during that period. After the period expires, Inkrail removes them when its next cleanup runs. Local recovery files, previous saved copies, old folders left after a library move, and any operating-system or cloud backups may remain separately. Removing the app does not necessarily delete those files.

You can access, export, or delete your local writing using Inkrail and your operating system's file manager. To remove all copies, also review old library locations and backups you control.

04 / BEYOND INKRAIL

Optional cloud folders and external services

Inkrail has no built-in cloud syncing. If you choose a folder managed by iCloud Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, or another sync provider, that provider may upload or process the folder under its own terms and privacy policy. This is separate from Inkrail; sync availability and reliability are not guaranteed by Inkrail.

The Inkrail website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Website visitors may make requests to GitHub-hosted assets and GitHub's releases API, and to other resources such as the Shields.io download-count badge. A YouTube player is requested only when a visitor chooses to watch the demonstration. These providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request data, under their own privacy policies. Downloading Inkrail through GitHub or obtaining it through a platform store is also subject to that platform's privacy practices.

05 / GETTING IN TOUCH

Support and communications

If you choose to contact the developer, the information you provide (for example, your email address, issue description, and any attachments) may be used to respond to your request and maintain the project. Please do not share private writing or sensitive personal information in public GitHub issues. Information you publish in a public issue is visible to others and handled by GitHub under its own terms.

06 / YOUR CHOICES

Security and control

Inkrail relies on your device's file permissions and any security or encryption provided by your operating system or chosen storage provider. Inkrail does not claim to offer end-to-end encryption for its local library. You should keep your device and backups secure, particularly if you store sensitive writing.

You control the storage folder, optional use of third-party sync, exports, and deletion of your local data. Because the developer does not maintain a cloud copy of your letters, we cannot retrieve or delete copies stored solely on your device or with your chosen storage provider.

07 / STAYING CURRENT

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Inkrail's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published here with a revised Last updated date. Material changes should be reflected in the policy before or when the relevant feature is released.
